I'm Tito Favaro

Senior Industrial Designer, robotics hardware. Barcelona.

I design humanoid and service robots from concept to real-world deployment. For the last eight years at PAL Robotics I have owned the exterior hardware of social, mobile-manipulation and bipedal robots, turning complex electromechanical systems into products that are manufacturable, serviceable and easy for people to live with.

Twenty years in industrial design, human factors and applied research, from HP's 3D printers to urban robots for the streets of Barcelona.

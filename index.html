Kangaroo

Bipedal Humanoid Platform

  • 2023–2026
  • Industrial Design Lead
  • CNC aluminium
Role
Industrial Design Lead. Hardware architecture, exterior design and mechanical integration.
Challenge
Kangaroo is the most mechanically complex platform at PAL Robotics. The design had to respect strict kinematic constraints while improving visual coherence and access to the internal hardware.
Contribution
  • Exterior hardware design and cosmetic refinement
  • Integration of sensors and mechanical subsystems
  • Collaboration with mechanical and electronics engineering teams
  • Design for Manufacturing for CNC aluminium and structural components
  • Better access for maintenance and assembly
Outcome
A visually coherent humanoid platform that balances complex bipedal mechanics with a clear robotic identity and improved serviceability.

Profile

Senior Industrial Designer specialising in robotics hardware and physical product development. I turn complex electromechanical systems into functional, manufacturable and user-centred products. My work spans humanoid and service robotics, advanced CAD surfacing, mechanical packaging, ergonomics and close collaboration with engineering, from early concepts and prototypes through to manufacturing.
